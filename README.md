# Jefa John

### Creative Technologist · Web Developer · Robotics Educator · Digital Media Professional

I'm a Kenya-based technology educator and creative professional working across web development, coding and robotics education, electronics, automation, and digital media.

I teach web development with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, and introduce learners to coding, robotics, and physical computing through practical, project-based learning. I also document and build hands-on technology projects that connect software with hardware.

## What I work on

- **Web development:** Building and teaching responsive websites and foundational front-end development with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- **Robotics and physical computing:** Arduino Uno, ESP32, sensors, actuators, electronics, and microcontroller-based projects.
- **Coding and technology education:** Developing learner-friendly lessons, projects, and teaching resources for coding and robotics.
- **Automation and IoT exploration:** Prototyping practical systems that use sensors, control logic, and electronic components.
- **Digital media and creative production:** Photography, videography, graphic design, video editing, and digital content.
